# Playtest plans

Each lane plays one plan. Copy a plan's text into the lane's brief with
`new-lane.sh`, or write a new one in the same voice: a goal, what is allowed,
and what is not. Vary plans across lanes; agents left to choose converge on
whatever worked last time.

## Forward rush

Win early. Attack as soon as you can field a force that can hurt the enemy's
base. Forward production near the enemy is allowed. Target whatever wins
fastest: workers, defenses or Foundries.

## Held army

Build a balanced army and armed defenses at home, but send no unit to attack and
build nothing outside your home area before 5:00 (tick 6000). Your home area is
the ground nearer the centre of your starting Foundry than the centre of any
enemy's starting Foundry; one unit at a time may scout beyond it. After 5:00,
attack to win.

## Harass

Keep the enemy economy under pressure from the first minutes with small, fast
raids on workers and Extractors, while building your own economy. Commit to a
main attack only once the raids have put you ahead.

## Air first

Open toward aircraft. Use air to scout, raid and break defenses, adding ground
units only as needed to hold your base and finish structures.

## Fast expansion

Take more Extractors than the enemy as early as you can, defend them, and win on
economy with a larger army later.

## Forward Fabricator

Build a Fabricator close to the enemy's base as early as you can, and fight from
it: train its units there and attack whatever wins fastest. Defend it while it
goes up.

## Siege

Win by breaking the enemy's base from range. Build toward artillery and a
spotter, hold the ground in front of your guns, and shell defenses and Foundries
from outside their reach.

## Lift drop

Win by landing an army where the enemy is weakest. Build toward air transport,
carry ground units past the enemy's defenses, and drop them on its workers,
Extractors or Foundries.

## Late tech

Defend with as little as you need while you tech toward the strongest late units
and weapons the rules offer, then win with them. Scout enough to know when the
enemy is coming.

## Any way to win

Win by whatever plan you think beats this opponent. Scout it, find what it does
badly, and use that. If your first plan fails, change it.

## Typical player

Play the way a typical decent player would: a sound economy, a mixed army, an
attack when you feel ahead. Do not hunt for tricks or exploit exact numbers;
make the plays a person who has played a few matches would make.

## Fortified tech

Play a patient game and never rush. Build a strong economy and fortify your main
base early: a Bastion, about three Turrets in front, two Flak Turrets, an Array
and a Repair Bay, upgraded as you can afford it. Expand behind your defenses,
tech toward the strongest units and structures, and attack only when you are
clearly ahead, with a large mixed army.

## Sharp player

Play as a strong, experienced player. Scout early and keep scouting, so you
learn what the opponent is doing before it reaches you, and answer what you see
at once rather than following a fixed build. Keep your economy growing behind
your answers, and attack when you are clearly ahead.

## Ready for a proxy

Play as a strong, experienced player who has seen this opponent build production
close to its enemy's base early. Scout your own side of the map as well as
theirs from the start, and be ready to answer forward production before it
fields an army. Keep your economy growing behind your answers, and attack when
you are clearly ahead.
