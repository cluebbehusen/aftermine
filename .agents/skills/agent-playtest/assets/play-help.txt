Playtest commands. `./play <command> --help` explains one.

  digest [--map]    What your seat knows now; --map adds a map.
  advance <ticks>   Run the game. Orders take effect from the next tick.
  status            The tick, and the result once the match ends.

Orders. Each names your seat first: ./play hunt 0 --units 4,5 --to 30,20
  run, hunt, advance-units, patrol, stop, attack-unit, attack-building,
  accompany, harvest, build, repair, repair-unit, salvage, cancel,
  upgrade, train, rally
  send              Any command with no verb of its own, as JSON.

An order the game would reject is refused at once with its reason, and
nothing is sent. Other commands of the debug client are not part of the
playtest.
