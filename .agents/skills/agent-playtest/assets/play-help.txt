Playtest commands. `./play <command> --help` explains one.

  digest [--map]    What your seat knows now; --map adds a map.
  advance <ticks>   Run the game, then list what your seat saw happen.
                    Orders take effect from the next tick. The advance
                    stops on the tick the match ends.
  status            The tick, and the result once the match ends.

Orders. Each names your seat first: ./play hunt @SEAT@ --units 4,5 --to 30,20
  run, hunt, advance-units, patrol, stop, attack-unit, attack-building,
  accompany, harvest, build, repair, repair-unit, salvage, cancel,
  upgrade, train, rally
  train             --count 1-5 queues several of one unit at once.
  attack-building   --target <id> while in sight, or --at x,y, its anchor
                    as the digest lists it, which also reaches a structure
                    remembered out of sight.
  harvest           --node x,y: a scrap tile, or a wreck's tile to collect
                    the wreck. salvage strips your own finished structure.
  send              Any command with no verb of its own, as JSON, such as
                    a Spire's strike once its round is ready:
                    ./play send @SEAT@ '{"type":"launch","building":12,"target":{"x":40,"y":20}}'

An order the game would reject is refused at once with its reason, and
that order is not sent. Other commands of the debug client are not part of
the playtest.

Order labels in the digest:
  idle          nothing to do; armed units fight what comes in range
  run           walking to a tile without fighting
  hunt          marching to a tile, fighting what it meets
  advance       moving to a tile, shooting only what is already in range
  attack        on one target until it is gone
  harvest       mining and hauling scrap
  return_cargo  taking carried scrap to a Foundry
  build         working an own site
  found         walking to place a deferred site; it turns into build once
                the whole footprint is in sight
  repair, repair_unit  welding a structure or unit on your order
  tend          welding on its own while idle (see rules.txt)
  accompany     escorting a unit
  salvage, board, unload, land, retire, scout  as their verbs say

Digest sections:
  ROUNDS FROM OUT OF SIGHT  direct rounds fired at what you see by a gun
                you do not: where each landed, the way it came from, what
                fired it, the first tile of its line you saw, and its tick
  reloading N   on a unit or structure line: the ticks its weapon still
                waits before it may fire, a/b for two weapons; absent once
                every weapon is ready
  MAGAZINES     each Spire or Lattice you know of: building is its id,
                loaded its rounds ready, assembly the ticks of work on the
                round being built (none while nothing is ordered); your own
                add how many are ordered and whether they refill on their own
